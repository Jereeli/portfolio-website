// CHESS PROJECT

CHESS PROJECT

A personal project in which I design, model, and 3D-print a full chess set — board and every piece. Each piece is intended to have a unique appearance and extra mechanical features. I'm also designing an Arduino-based circuit to embed in the board so it can detect piece movements and track player times (using strain gauges and an ESP32 microcontroller with weighted pieces).

KNIGHT (RATSU)

Modeled and designed in Siemens NX. Features a simple mechanism: when the piece is lifted, the horse's legs extend outward via a shaft — and retract when placed back on the board.

// PRODUCT DESIGN

NOISE-CANCELING SPEAKER

In a product design course, my task was to design a new technological product with market demand. My concept: a smart speaker sold to property owners or businesses near constant low-frequency noise sources (railways, airports, amusement parks). The speaker cancels repetitive low-frequency rumble using AI, a PID controller, and active noise cancellation.
